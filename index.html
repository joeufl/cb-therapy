Welcome

I'm Catherine Bush, a native English-speaking psychotherapist in specialist training under teaching supervision (Psychotherapeutin in Fachausbildung unter Lehrsupervision), offering individual, couple, and family therapy in Vienna.

My work is grounded in systemic family therapy and in the understanding that the difficulties we experience rarely exist in isolation. How we feel, relate, and respond to the world is shaped by our relationships, our histories, the roles we have taken on, and the systems we are part of.

In therapy, I am interested not only in what is difficult, but also in how it came to make sense. Together, we can look at recurring patterns, relationships, and ways of understanding yourself that may once have been useful but no longer serve you in the same way.

I see therapy as a collaborative process rather than a place where someone else tells you who you are or what you should do. My role is to offer a space for curiosity, reflection, and new perspectives — perhaps even one in which different ways of relating to yourself and to others can become possible.

How I can support you

I work with people navigating anxiety, depression, ADHD, relationship difficulties, periods of change or uncertainty, questions of identity and belonging, recurring interpersonal patterns, and the wish to understand themselves and their relationships more deeply.

About me

I was born and raised in the United States and have been living in Austria for several years. Through family ties to Germany, I also grew up speaking German, which has shaped my connection to both cultures and languages.

Before beginning my psychotherapy training, I studied music education in the U.S. and later worked as an English teaching assistant in Austria. That experience helped me develop strong communication skills, patience, and confidence in working closely with people from different backgrounds.

I had wanted to become a therapist for several years before formally starting this path. I am especially interested in working with people experiencing anxiety, depression, emotional overwhelm, and difficult life transitions, as well as with couples who feel they have lost their way with one another.

People close to me often describe me as warm, thoughtful, curious, open-minded, and a good listener. I value honesty, humor, and creating a space where people can speak openly without feeling judged or pressured.

Having built a life in another country myself, I also understand some of the challenges that can come with living between cultures, languages, and different expectations. This is something I bring with me when working with English-speaking and international clients in Austria.
