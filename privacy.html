Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Protecting your personal data is important to me. I process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG) and the relevant professional regulations.

This Privacy Policy explains how your data is processed when you visit www.catherinebush.at, use the embedded online appointment booking system or contact me by email.

My website uses technically necessary cookies and similar technologies that may be required for its operation, security or basic functionality.

Wix and the website features enabled may also use analytics or other non-essential technologies.

Where consent is required by law, these technologies are used only after you have given your prior consent. The legal basis for the associated processing of personal data is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Technically necessary data processing is carried out in particular on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Online appointment booking through TheraPsy Bookings

An online appointment booking calendar provided by TheraPsy Bookings is embedded on my website as an iframe. The calendar allows you to select and request an appointment for an initial psychotherapeutic consultation.

TheraSoft GmbH
Pachergasse 17/Top 11
4400 Steyr
Email: kontakt@therapsy.at

Processing when the calendar loads

Because the booking calendar is embedded as an external element, a technical connection to TheraPsy Bookings is established when the calendar loads.

The following technical data may be transmitted or processed:

  • Operating system
  • Date and time of the server request

This processing is for the technical provision of the booking calendar.

According to the provider, TheraPsy Bookings does not use tracking services, third-party tracking scripts or cookies. The application is reportedly operated on Amazon Web Services server infrastructure within the European Union.

Processing when an appointment is booked

If you request an appointment through the calendar, the following data in particular is processed:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Selected appointment time

The data is processed to handle your appointment request, organise the appointment and communicate with you about it.

The legal basis for processing personal data required to arrange an appointment is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

If health data is revealed through the booking or additional information and is processed, an additional legal basis under Article 9(2) GDPR is required.

Please do not enter detailed information about diagnoses, health conditions or other particularly sensitive circumstances when booking online.

According to the information published by TheraPsy Bookings, booking requests are generally stored in the booking system until the appointment and then automatically deleted. Any further processing required for appointment administration, treatment records or statutory retention obligations is unaffected.

Further information about the provider's data processing is available at: https://www.therapsy.at/datenschutzerklaerungen/bookings

5. Contact by email

If you contact me by email, I process the information you provide voluntarily, in particular your name, email address and the content of your message.

I process this information to respond to your enquiry, communicate with you and, where appropriate, arrange an appointment.

For enquiries about arranging treatment, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other enquiries, processing may be based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

If health data is processed, an additional legal basis under Article 9(2) GDPR is required.

I use Proton Mail for my professional email communications.

Please note that the confidentiality of an email transmission also depends on your email provider and the security settings in use.

For this reason, I recommend that you do not send detailed health information or sensitive medical documents by ordinary email when making an initial enquiry.

Enquiries that do not lead to treatment are deleted once continued storage is no longer necessary, unless statutory retention obligations or other lawful reasons apply.

6. Processing of treatment data

In the course of psychotherapeutic treatment, personal data — including special categories of personal data, particularly health data — is processed.

This is for psychotherapeutic treatment, appointment administration, legally required documentation and billing.

Processing is based on the applicable provisions of Article 6 GDPR and, for health data, in particular Article 9(2)(h) GDPR in conjunction with Austrian professional law.

Psychotherapeutic records are maintained using the TheraPsy practice software on a MacBook used exclusively by me. Backup files of the locally stored records are kept in Proton Drive. Signed agreements and other paper documents are kept in a locked filing cabinet at the practice.

As a psychotherapist in specialist training under teaching supervision, I am subject to the professional duty of confidentiality under Section 45 of the Psychotherapy Act 2024.

Teaching supervision is organized as part of my training at Sigmund Freud Private University and is conducted in accordance with applicable confidentiality and data protection obligations.

Under Section 44 of the PThG 2024, psychotherapeutic records are generally retained for ten years after treatment ends.

Patients receive separate privacy information about the processing of their treatment data before treatment begins.

7. Recipients and processors

Personal data is processed or disclosed to third parties only to the extent necessary and permitted by law.

The technical service providers used include, in particular:

  • Wix, for website provision and technical operation
  • TheraPsy Bookings, for online appointment administration
  • Proton Mail, for professional email communications
  • Proton Drive, for encrypted backups

Where service providers process personal data as processors, they are engaged in accordance with Article 28 GDPR.

Confidential treatment information is disclosed to other people or organisations only where there is an appropriate legal basis.

8. Storage periods

Personal data is stored only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose or as required by statutory retention periods.

The following criteria apply in particular:

  • Website access data: Retained in accordance with the hosting provider's technically necessary and legally permitted logging periods.
  • Appointment requests: Retained in the booking system in accordance with the provider's published deletion procedures. If data is transferred into other lawful documentation processes, the retention periods applicable to those processes apply.
  • Email enquiries: Retained until the enquiry has been fully handled and beyond that only where there is an appropriate legal basis.
  • Psychotherapeutic records: Retained for ten years after treatment ends under Section 44 of the PThG 2024.

  • Right of access under Article 15 GDPR
  • Right to rectification under Article 16 GDPR
  • Right to erasure under Article 17 GDPR
  • Right to restriction of processing under Article 18 GDPR
  • Right to data portability under Article 20 GDPR
  • Right to object under Article 21 GDPR
  • Right to withdraw consent under Article 7(3) GDPR

To exercise your rights, you can contact me at praxis@catherinebush.at.

Please note that statutory retention obligations or other lawful reasons for continued storage may apply in individual cases.

10. Whether providing data is required

You do not need to provide your name or contact details simply to visit the website. However, technically necessary connection data may be processed automatically.

To book an appointment online, you must provide at least the personal data needed to identify and handle your appointment request.

If you do not provide this information, you may be unable to book online.

You are not required to provide detailed health information when booking an appointment.

11. Automated decision-making

I do not use automated decision-making, including profiling within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR, when handling appointment requests or making psychotherapeutic treatment decisions.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy will be updated if the website's technical features, service providers, data processing or legal requirements change.
